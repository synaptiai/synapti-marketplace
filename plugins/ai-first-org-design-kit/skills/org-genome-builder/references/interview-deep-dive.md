# Organizational Genome Builder — Deep-Dive Interview Questions

Use when the standard 11 questions don't surface enough depth. Organized by genome section.

## Identity Deep Dives

### Mission Pressure-Testing
- "If you had to kill half your product/service lines tomorrow, which would survive? Why those?"
- "What's the one thing you do better than anyone else? Not 'differently' — better."
- "If a competitor copied everything you do, what would they still be missing?"

### Values Stress-Testing
- "Tell me about a time your values cost you money. What happened and would you do it again?"
- "When hiring, what's the thing that makes you say 'absolutely not' regardless of how talented the candidate is?"
- "What behavior do you tolerate in your best performer that you wouldn't tolerate in anyone else? That's your real value hierarchy."

### Voice & Tone Extraction
- "Show me a customer email you're proud of. Now show me one that makes you cringe. What's the difference?"
- "If your organization were a person at a dinner party, how would they talk? Who would they talk to?"
- "What's a phrase your team uses that an outsider wouldn't understand? Why does it matter?"

## Decision Architecture Deep Dives

### Authority Boundary Finding
- "What's the most expensive decision someone on your team has made without asking permission? Was that okay?"
- "Walk me through a decision that took way too long. Where did it get stuck? Who held it up? Why?"
- "Is there a decision that SHOULD require approval but doesn't? What's the risk you're carrying?"

### Tradeoff Excavation
- "When speed and quality collide — and they always do — what actually happens? Not the policy. The reality."
- "When a customer asks for something that conflicts with your product vision, who wins? Show me a recent example."
- "What's a tradeoff you made in the last month that you're still not sure was right?"

## Quality Standards Deep Dives

### Taste Articulation
- "Show me something your team produced that's a 9/10 in your eyes. Now a 6/10. What's the difference between them?"
- "When you review someone's work, what's the first thing you look at? What makes you stop reading?"
- "If you had to train a new hire to have your taste in one hour, what would you tell them?"

### Anti-Pattern Discovery
- "What's the most common mistake people make when they first join? The thing that's 'technically correct but not how we do it'?"
- "Show me an output that an outsider would think is fine but that bothers you. What specifically bothers you?"
- "What's the 'tell' that reveals someone used a template instead of thinking? What does thoughtful work look like vs. template work?"

## Governance Boundary Finding

### Nightmare Scenario Mapping
- "What's the worst thing that happened because someone acted without checking? How did you prevent it from happening again?"
- "If an agent had full access to your systems for 24 hours with no oversight, what's the first thing that could go wrong?"
- "What's the thing you check last thing at night / first thing in the morning? That's your real anxiety — encode it."

### Escalation Pattern Discovery
- "When something goes sideways, who's the first person people call? Why that person?"
- "Is there a situation where 'ask forgiveness, not permission' is acceptable in your org? Where's the line?"
- "What information would you need to see to approve something in 30 seconds vs. needing a meeting?"

## For Complex / Multi-Team Organizations

### Cross-Team Dynamics
- "Where do teams have different definitions of 'done'? How does that cause friction?"
- "Which team's quality standard sets the bar for the whole org? Does everyone agree it should?"
- "If two teams disagree on approach, how is that resolved? By whom? How long does it take?"

### Institutional Memory
- "What important knowledge exists only in one person's head? What happens when they're on vacation?"
- "What's a lesson the organization learned the hard way and then forgot? How did you end up re-learning it?"
- "Where are the 'we tried that already' stories? Are they documented or just oral history?"
