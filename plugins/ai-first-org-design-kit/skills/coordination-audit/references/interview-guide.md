# Coordination Audit — Extended Interview Guide

Use these when the standard 5 questions don't surface enough detail.

## Workflow Deep-Dive Questions

- "Walk me through the last thing that took way longer than it should have. Where did it get stuck?"
- "If you could eliminate one recurring meeting tomorrow with no consequences, which one?"
- "When something urgent comes in, how does it move through the organization? How is that different from the normal path?"
- "What information do you regularly need that's hard to get? Who has it, and what do you have to do to get it?"
- "Where do you see the same information being communicated multiple times to different people?"

## Approval Chain Diagnostics

- "For this approval: has it ever caught something that would have been a genuine problem? How often?"
- "What's the average wait time at this approval step? What's the worst case?"
- "If the approver is on vacation, what happens? Is there a backup? How often does work just wait?"
- "Is there an informal way people bypass this approval? How common is it?"

## Cultural Function Detection

- "Which meetings do people actually look forward to? Why?"
- "Where do people build relationships at work? Is it through the work itself or separate activities?"
- "If you automated all status-sharing, what would people miss? Not the information — the interaction."
- "Who are the people that 'hold the team together'? What do they actually do that creates that effect?"

## Power Structure Mapping

- "Who can say 'no' and make it stick?"
- "Who has information that nobody else has? How does that affect their influence?"
- "Whose opinion gets deferred to even when they're not the most senior person?"
- "Where does informal influence override formal authority?"

## Symptom-to-Root-Cause Guide

| Symptom | Likely Root Cause | Investigate |
|---------|------------------|-------------|
| "Everything takes forever" | Coordination overhead, too many handoffs | Map the handoff chain |
| "Nobody knows what's going on" | Information silos, no shared dashboard | Identify information monopolies |
| "We keep having the same discussions" | No decision recording, no institutional memory | Check for decision ledger |
| "People do things differently" | No specification, implicit quality standards | Check for documented standards |
| "We're always in meetings" | Coordination done through meetings, not infrastructure | Calculate meeting cost |
| "Approvals are bottlenecked" | Single-point-of-failure approvers, no quality gates | Map approval chain latency |
