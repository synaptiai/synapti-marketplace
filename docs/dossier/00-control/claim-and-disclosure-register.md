# Claim and Disclosure Register
<!-- contract: references/package-contract-00-control.md#claim-and-disclosure-register -->

## Method

Every sentence in `06-public/**` that makes a claim maps to one `approved` row below, matching its `Proposed wording` verbatim. Column definitions and identifier grammar are in the plugin reference `references/register-schemas.md`.

The package cannot approve its own claims. Where human approval is required by the disclosure policy and has not been recorded, `Status` is `pending` and the sentence does not ship. Zero unsupported public claims is a release gate.

**Disclosure policy in force:** `public`. The repository is public, the manifest is public, and this package is committed to the same public repository. `confidentiality` defaults to `Public`.

**Approval authority:** `disclosure.publicClaimApproval` is `required`. Daniel Bentes is the sole approver for every claim type.

The register carries 67 rows: 52 approved, 2 held, 13 superseded.

Thirty-five were approved on 2026-07-26 in the session that produced this package. Thirteen of those have since been superseded — ten by claims the 7e4a097..7ee4923 range falsified, CL-0009 by the removal of the tree's only dependency manifest, CL-0022 with the claim it qualified, and CL-0048 by an approved successor that states the same fact in a form an operator can act on.

On 2026-09-10 two rounds of approval ran. Twelve replacement and new claims were presented individually and ten approved; CL-0043 (maintainer concentration) and CL-0047 (absence of a vulnerability scan) were held. Then an independent audit found that between 5 and 61 claim-bearing sentences in the public guides mapped to no row at all — the registration check that should have caught them cannot read bullets or table cells (issue #176). Nineteen further claims were drafted from executed evidence and approved: four registering published sentences that survived checking, eight replacing published sentences that turned out false, and seven disclosing things the guides had not said.

Four existing rows were re-scoped in the same pass. CL-0046 was true of this branch and false at the product version the guides advertise. CL-0041 asserted install reproducibility where the evidence supports only what the manifest contains. CL-0031's scope now excludes the sentence naming the guide's own audience, which is not a claim about the project. CL-0016 carries its observation date into the public sentence.

The eight replaced sentences are worth naming as a group, because every replacement is narrower and less flattering than what it replaced: helper-script exit codes, scaffolding idempotency, timeouts, release bundles, bash-3.2 coverage, the executable-bit assertion, network transmission, and the existence of a behavioural evaluation. A package that only ever corrects upward is not being checked.

**A pending claim does not ship.** Where a held claim replaced an approved one, the subject leaves the public documents entirely rather than reverting to the superseded wording.
